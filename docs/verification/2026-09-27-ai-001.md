# AI-001 Verification — 2026-09-27

Scope: AI recipe-draft workflow, quotas and budget reservation (AC-06, AC-13, AC-02 for drafts), with the deterministic mock provider. Branch `ai-001`. Real-provider evaluation has not run yet (needs the owner's keys).

## Acceptance criteria (set before changes)

1. A draft never reaches the menu until a member saves it; discarding changes nothing; saving twice (sequentially or concurrently) creates one recipe; a discarded draft cannot be saved (AC-06).
2. Concurrent requests cannot exceed the household limit, the personal attempt limit or the monthly budget; a timed-out call keeps its full reservation (AC-13).
3. With AI off, exhausted or failing, manual recipe entry keeps working and the UI offers it (AC-13).
4. Another household or a removed member cannot read, save or discard a draft (AC-02).
5. A killed process leaves a failed, not a spinning, request; nothing is retried blindly.
6. Only the dish name and preferences are sent to the provider.
