## Back navigation follow-up (owner report, 2026-10-03)

After PR #31, the owner reported that Back from Add recipe or AI drafting returned to Home instead of Recipes. Cause: those views, the categories view, the recipe card and the order editor were in-page state without history entries, so Back left the whole page. Fix on `claude/ux-004-back-navigation`:

- `useViewHistory` gives each in-place view a history entry. The page's Back, the browser's Back and a phone's swipe close one view at a time: a reviewed AI draft returns to AI drafting, then to Recipes. Closing a view with its own button (Cancel, Save) removes the entry, so no extra Back step is left behind.
- "Add more dishes" on the confirmation page is a real Back, landing on the dishes. A placed order replaces the confirmation page in history, so Back from Orders returns to Home instead of an empty confirmation page.
- `navigation.spec.ts` (3 cases × desktop/mobile) covers:
  - the page's Back on the recipe form;
  - browser Back on AI drafting, categories and the recipe card;
  - no extra entry after Cancel;
  - AI review → AI drafting → Recipes;
  - the order editor;
  - skipping the placed order's confirmation.
- Full `npm run check` passed: 90 integration, 90 browser cases.
