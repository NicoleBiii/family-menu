**Chicken Stir-Fry** — Quick and delicious stir-fried chicken with vegetables and a savory sauce. (serves 4)

- 500 g Chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 piece Bell peppers, sliced
- 1 piece Onion, sliced
- 3 clove Garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp Ginger, minced
- 3 tbsp Soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp Sesame oil
- 2 tbsp Vegetable oil
- 200 g Broccoli, florets
- 150 g Snap peas
- 120 ml Water
- 1 tbsp Cornstarch
- 2 piece Green onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp Sesame seeds

1. Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat until it shimmers.
2. Add chicken pieces and cook until golden and cooked through, about 6-8 minutes. Remove and set aside.
3. Add more oil if needed, then stir-fry garlic and ginger for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add onion and bell peppers, stir-fry for 2-3 minutes until slightly softened.
5. Add broccoli and snap peas, continue stir-frying for 3-4 minutes.
6. Mix water and cornstarch together in a small bowl to create a slurry.
7. Return chicken to the wok and add soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil.
8. Pour in the cornstarch mixture and stir constantly for 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens.
9. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
10. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds before serving.
11. Serve hot over steamed rice or noodles.

**Banana Bread** — A moist and tender loaf cake made with ripe bananas, perfect for breakfast or a sweet snack. (serves 8)

- 3 piece ripe bananas, mashed
- 280 g all-purpose flour
- 150 g sugar
- 100 g butter, softened
- 2 piece eggs
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 60 ml whole milk

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and grease a loaf tin.
2. Cream together the softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy, about 2-3 minutes.
3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then add the vanilla extract.
4. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt.
5. Mash the ripe bananas until mostly smooth, leaving some small chunks for texture.
6. Fold the mashed bananas and dry ingredients alternately into the butter mixture, starting and ending with the dry ingredients.
7. Add the milk and gently mix until just combined; do not overmix.
8. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf tin and smooth the top.
9. Bake for 50-60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean or with just a few moist crumbs.
10. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.
