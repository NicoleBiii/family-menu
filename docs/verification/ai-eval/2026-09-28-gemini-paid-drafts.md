**Classic Chicken Stir-Fry** — A quick and vibrant stir-fry featuring tender chicken strips and crisp vegetables tossed in a savory sauce. (serves 4)

- 500 g Chicken breast, sliced
- 200 g Broccoli, florets
- 2 piece Bell pepper, sliced
- 1 piece Carrot, julienned
- 60 ml Soy sauce
- 3 clove Garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp Ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp Vegetable oil
- 1 tsp Sesame oil
- 1 tbsp Cornstarch

1. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, grated ginger, minced garlic, sesame oil, and cornstarch.
2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
3. Add the sliced chicken breast to the pan and cook until browned and cooked through, about 5-6 minutes.
4. Remove the chicken from the pan and set aside.
5. Add the broccoli, bell pepper, and carrots to the same hot pan and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
6. Return the cooked chicken to the pan with the vegetables.
7. Pour the sauce mixture over the ingredients and toss everything together for 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens.
8. Serve immediately, preferably over steamed rice.

**Spaghetti Bolognese** — A classic Italian-inspired pasta dish featuring a rich, slow-cooked meat sauce served over spaghetti. (serves 4)

- 400 g spaghetti
- 500 g lean ground beef
- 1 piece onion, finely chopped
- 2 clove garlic, minced
- 1 piece carrot, finely diced
- 1 piece celery, finely diced
- 700 ml tomato passata
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 0.5 tsp black pepper (to taste)

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat and sauté the onion, carrot, and celery until softened.
2. Add the minced garlic and cook for one minute until fragrant.
3. Increase heat to medium-high, add the ground beef, and cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon.
4. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for two minutes.
5. Pour in the tomato passata and add the dried oregano, stirring well to combine.
6. Reduce heat to low and simmer the sauce gently for at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. While the sauce simmers, bring a large pot of water to a boil and cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
8. Drain the pasta and toss with the meat sauce before serving.

**Creamy Butternut Squash Soup** — A comforting and smooth velvety soup made with roasted butternut squash and aromatic herbs. (serves 4)

- 1 piece butternut squash, peeled and cubed (approx 1kg)
- 1 piece onion, chopped
- 2 clove garlic, minced
- 750 ml vegetable broth
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp thyme, dried
- salt (to taste)
- black pepper (to taste)

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onion and sauté for about 5 minutes until soft and translucent.
3. Add the minced garlic and thyme, cooking for another minute until fragrant.
4. Add the cubed butternut squash and stir to coat with the oil and spices.
5. Pour in the vegetable broth and bring the mixture to a boil.
6. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes until the squash is very tender.
7. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
8. Use an immersion blender to puree the soup until completely smooth.
9. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve warm.

**Banana Bread** — A classic, moist banana bread that is perfect for using up overripe bananas. (serves 8)

- 3 piece bananas, mashed
- 75 g butter, melted
- 150 g sugar
- 1 piece egg, beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 pinch salt
- 190 g all-purpose flour

1. Preheat your oven to 175C and grease a standard loaf pan.
2. In a large mixing bowl, stir together the melted butter and the sugar.
3. Mix in the egg, vanilla extract, and mashed bananas.
4. Sprinkle in the baking soda and salt, then stir to combine.
5. Add the flour and mix until just incorporated; do not overmix.
6. Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan.
7. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean.
8. Remove from the oven and let cool in the pan for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
