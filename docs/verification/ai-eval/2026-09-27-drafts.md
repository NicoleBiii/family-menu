**Chicken Stir-Fry** — Quick and vibrant stir-fried chicken with colorful vegetables in a savory sauce. (serves 4)

- 500 g Chicken breast, diced
- 2 piece Bell pepper, sliced
- 250 g Broccoli, florets
- 150 g Carrots, julienned
- 3 clove Garlic, minced
- 15 g Ginger, minced
- 3 tbsp Soy sauce
- 2 tbsp Oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp Sesame oil
- 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
- 1 tsp Sugar
- 1 tbsp Cornstarch (mixed with 2 tbsp water)
- 2 tbsp Vegetable oil (for cooking)
- 2 piece Spring onion, sliced (optional)

1. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, sugar, and cornstarch slurry to make the sauce.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat until very hot.
3. Add the diced chicken and stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until cooked through and golden. Remove and set aside.
4. Add the remaining oil to the wok, then add garlic and ginger. Stir-fry for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Add the harder vegetables first: carrots and broccoli. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until slightly tender.
6. Add the bell pepper slices and stir-fry for another 2 minutes.
7. Return the cooked chicken to the wok with all the vegetables.
8. Pour in the prepared sauce and toss everything together for 1-2 minutes until the sauce coats all ingredients and thickens slightly.
9. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Garnish with spring onion if desired.
10. Serve immediately over steamed rice or noodles.

- 280 g all-purpose flour
- 200 g sugar
- 100 g butter, softened
- 2 piece eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 100 g walnuts, chopped (optional)

2. In a bowl, mash the ripe bananas until mostly smooth.
3. Cream together the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then add vanilla extract.
5. Stir in the mashed bananas.
6. In another bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
7. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined.
8. Stir in the chopped walnuts if using.
9. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
10. Bake for 50-60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
11. Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes.
12. Turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.
